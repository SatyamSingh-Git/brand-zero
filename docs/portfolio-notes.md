## Discrepancies — do not paper over these

- **Revenue.** The site says ₹400 Cr+ annual. Tracxn reports ₹47.2 Cr for
  *Think9 Consumer Technologies Private Limited* for FY ending 31 Mar 2025.
  These are almost certainly different scopes (portfolio-wide vs one legal
  entity). Neither number goes in the deck as a hard figure.
- **Multiple entities.** Think9 Digital Consumer Brands Pvt Ltd and Think9
  Consumer Technologies Pvt Ltd both exist. Separately, **Think9 Founders
  Fund** at think9.vc is a different site and must not be conflated with the
  venture studio at think9co.in.
- **"30+ brands."** This comes from the challenge brief, not from any public
  Think9 source. Treat it as stated ambition.

## What this changes in the pitch

**1. Cold start is now a sourced number, not a guess.** Their own site says
11 ventures; the brief says they are building 30+. So the honest framing is
that Brand Zero is being proposed at brand 11, not brand 30 — and the slide
has to show what it does at 11. That is a much better slide than one that
assumes the end state.

**2. "Knowing when not to transfer" stops being a feature and becomes the
thesis.** Look at the actual mix: probiotics, protein, ayurveda, skincare,
gourmet food retail, an experiential retail format, kids publishing IP, and
apparel. There is no shared price-elasticity structure across comics and
probiotics. A single pooled "Think9 Brain" over this portfolio would be
confidently wrong — which is precisely the 0–5% coverage failure the backtest
already demonstrates. The heterogeneity of their real portfolio is the
strongest available argument for the three-level model.

**3. Broadway and Brand Bridge are a cold-start asset nobody else will
name.** Broadway hosts 350+ third-party brands; Brand Bridge reaches 10k+
modern trade stores. Those are observation surfaces on brands Think9 does not
own. A prior for brand 12 does not have to come only from the 11 owned
brands.

**4. Audience.** Santosh Desai and the Biyanis are consumer-culture and
retail operators, not quants. Lead with the thesis and operator language;
keep the mathematics reachable but not front-loaded. The interval-coverage
table is a backup slide, not slide 4.
